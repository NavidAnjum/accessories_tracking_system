ED Module / Order to Export Pack

One flow from buyer request to factory release.

This page maps the real working scenario: Buyer gives the order, Marketing receives it, Commercial checks PI and LC, Marketing approves, Factory starts production, and the final invoice, packing, delivery, and export papers are issued from the same chain.

Buyer Marketing Commercial PI LC Factory

Core line

Buyer -> Marketing -> Commercial -> PI + LC Check -> Marketing Approval -> Factory -> Delivery / Invoice / Packing

01

Buyer Sends Order

The process starts when the buyer places the order and shares requirements.

02

Marketing Receives

Marketing collects order details, buyer reference, timing, and production needs.

03

Commercial Reviews

Commercial checks PI, LC, item lines, prices, and document readiness.

04

Marketing Approves

Marketing verifies the commercial output before the order moves to factory.

05

Factory Starts

Once approved, the order goes to production and then to dispatch and export paperwork.

Main understanding

The workbook is a document pack, not a single form.

Sales order data comes from another system. Commercial turns that into PI and shipment documents. Later forms reuse the same commercial data instead of starting from scratch.

System trigger

Sales Order is the starting input.

The external sales-order report already contains the strongest auto-fill data: sales order number, buyer, item lines, quantity, price, amount, PI number, DC number, shipment dates, and receivables invoice details.

Department flow

Who does what

Buyer

Gives the order and business requirement.

Marketing

Receives the order, passes it to Commercial, and checks the final draft.

Commercial

Checks PI, checks LC, and prepares invoice, packing, and delivery documents.

Factory

Starts production only after the order is checked and approved.

Approval route

PI and LC sit before factory release.

  1. Buyer gives order
  2. Marketing receives order
  3. Marketing sends details to Commercial
  4. Commercial creates or confirms PI
  5. Commercial checks LC
  6. Marketing checks commercial details
  7. Approved order goes to Factory
  8. Production, delivery, invoice, and packing follow

Document chain

How one approved order becomes many forms.

Source

Sales Order System

Brings buyer, line items, qty, price, amount, PI number, DC number, and dates.

Commercial base

PI / Commercial Invoice

Main checked sheet where the commercial order view becomes usable for the rest of the pack.

Operational forms

Packing + Delivery + Truck Challan

Used for packing confirmation, dispatch, transport handover, and factory-side execution.

Export forms

Certificate + Beneficiary + Bill of Exchange

Created after commercial details are checked, especially for bank and export submission.

Final pack

Forwarding

The cover sheet for the full negotiated document set.

Auto-fill data

What should come from the system

  • Sales Order Number
  • Customer PO Number
  • Buyer
  • Item code and item description
  • UOM, quantity, price, amount
  • PI number and DC number
  • Requested, shipment, and dispatch dates
  • Receivables invoice details

Manual or master data

What Commercial still needs to complete

  • Consignee address
  • Bank address and bank wording
  • Applicant IRC, TIN, VAT, BIN
  • Beneficiary VAT or BIN
  • Bond license and HS code
  • Sales contract number
  • Export certificate wording
  • Bill of exchange text and bank narrative

One-line summary

Sales Order feeds Commercial. Commercial feeds approval. Approval releases Factory. Factory triggers delivery and the final export pack.
