## Customer Approval Flow

```
Marketing creates customer  →  Team Leader (Tanvir) approves  →  Finance  →  Commercial  →  Completed
```

- A marketing user submits a new customer profile.
- It goes to the **Team Leader** stage first — Tanvir reviews & signs.
- Then it moves through Finance and Commercial before it is marked Completed.
